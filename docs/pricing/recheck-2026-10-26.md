## Recheck protocol

For each unresolved rate, capture:

1. Exact product name, brand, pack size and SKU where available.
2. Named outlet and branch/area, or the exact online seller.
3. Price in ZAR, whether it is per unit or case, and case quantity.
4. Observation date, promo validity/expiry, stock status and delivery fee.
5. Source URL or a dated shelf/quote photo.
6. Whether the item is a retail baseline, supplier sourcing cost, or iCamagu's founder-set selling price.

The founder's bulk-buying strategy remains separate from these reference observations: target at least **5% below comparable retail**, with an **8–10% target at the qualifying larger combined-volume tier**, subject to the relevant outlet's eligible products and negotiated terms. This policy does not justify fabricating or silently refreshing any particular shelf price.
