# isiXhosa: lines to review

Every isiXhosa line in the app, beside the English it replaces. These are a
draft, written without a first-language speaker. Please correct anything that
is wrong, stiff or not how people in the Eastern Cape actually speak, in the
last column, and it will be changed in `src/i18n/xh.js`.

## Decided by the founder (22 Nov 2026)

- A ceremony is **umsebenzi / imisebenzi** throughout (not umsitho).
- Stokvels are **Imigalelo**, Bulk Buy is **Thenga**.
- Months use the traditional names (EyoMqungu ... EyoMnga), with the English
  month written small beside them (as text inside a sentence: in brackets).

## Not translated on purpose

- The guidance in the mother's guide on ulwaluko: only its headings are
  translated. The guidance is too important to guess; it should be written
  by someone you trust.
- The steps and items inside each ceremony plan ("Written consent from his
  parent or guardian") are stored on the server and are a separate piece of
  work.

`{n}`, `{title}`, `{tab}`, `{date}` and similar are filled in by the app: keep
them as they are.

## Money words (used the same everywhere)

Stokvel **umgalelo** · contribution **umnikelo** · payout **intlawulo** · official **igosa** · rotation order **ulandelelwano** · group order (pool) **iqela lokuthenga** · merchant **umthengisi**. If any of these is not the word members use, change it here once and it changes on every screen.
